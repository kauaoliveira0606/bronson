How To Read Your Numbers

Profit Per Unit

What you keep from one sale after every cost per unit.

It is your selling price minus your total cost.

Margin

The share of each sale you keep as profit.

It is profit per unit divided by your selling price.

ROI

What you make back on the money you put in.

It is net profit divided by total costs.

MRR

Monthly recurring revenue: what your active subscribers pay you every month.

Max MRR is where it levels off, when new customers only replace the ones who cancel.

Renewal Rate

The share of subscribers who pay you again next month.

It sets how many orders you get per customer, so small changes add up.

AOV

Average order value: what a customer pays per order on average.

Lifetime revenue is AOV times orders per customer. Lifetime profit only counts ad cost on the first order.
