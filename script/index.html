Stage 1
Warm Up the Lead
⚠️ Tonality — Whole Call
Concerned "homie" tone from the first hello. Curious, calm, like a friend — never salesy. Sound like a salesperson and they close up.
⚡ Pace — Speed = Authority
Keep it moving or they tune out / it feels like therapy. Fast + clear up top, keep the energy up. Slow down ONLY in the pain.
Opening
"Hey, is this [Name]?

What's going on [Name]? This is [Rep] from Bronson's team here. I saw you applied to get started with AI ecom, is that correct?"
PACE: Speed UP the first 10 sec but be crystal clear who/why/what — don't sound like every other cold caller or they tune out. PAIN is most important later: don't just chase goal-state — build the burning building (current situation → "how's that affecting other areas of your life?") then raise their vibrations.
Interest Question
"Awesome, we'll get you access to everything here in a sec. Just kinda walk me through, [Name] — what exactly got you interested in joining the free course and learning how to build an AI ecom brand?"
Responses — pick the matching path:
Escape the Matrix
Making money, replace job, freedom, new income, side hustle
Vague / Just Looking
Just checking it out, not sure yet, looking for info
B2B / Scale Business
Has a business, wants more clients, grow company
If: Escape the Matrix
"Ok, and I'm sure you're aware there's a lot of business models out there. I guess, what did you see in Bronson's content that made you feel like AI ecom would be the right fit for you?"
★ Get them to articulate WHY this business model. Their answer tells you how aware they are of what AI ecom actually is.
If: Vague / Just Looking
"Got you. People typically request the free course for one of two reasons: they're either looking for another source of income, or they're already doing AI ecom and just looking to get to that next level. Which one of those boats do you feel like you're in personally?"
★ "Just looking for info" people don't buy. This question forces them to pick a lane. Whatever they pick, follow that path.
If: B2B / Scale Business
"Awesome, and I'm sure you're aware that there are a lot of tools and courses out there in this industry. I guess, what did you see in Bronson's content that made you want to apply to work with him specifically?"
Stage 2
Current State
"Ok, well just so I could understand your current situation a little better — what exactly are you doing for work right now?"
No Job
Has a Job
If No Job
"Ok, and I guess what's the goal here with AI ecom? Are you hoping this could become your main source of income, or what does that look like?"
If Has a Job
"Oh, you're doing [JOB]. And I guess, what would be the goal here with AI ecom? Are you hoping this could replace your [JOB], or what does that look like?"
★ We need to know: do they want to REPLACE their situation or ADD to it? This determines how deep we go on problem awareness.
Stage 3
Problem Awareness
Path 1 — "Yes, I Want Out"  ★ Easy
"Ok, and I guess — what's causing you to want to get out of your [JOB], bro?"
"And when you say [THEIR WORD] — can you give me an example? What's that actually look like day to day?"
★ SEE: Get the specific event or reality they experience. "My back is messed up from lifting equipment."
"And what's that actually like to deal with day to day?"
Now go for the FEEL, not another example. Don't ask "what negative impact" — too salesy on low-ticket. Mirror their situation back and pull the emotion. Keep probing until you get how it actually feels.
★ FEEL: Get the negative emotion. "I can't sleep, I never recover, I go right back to work."
Path 2 — "Job's Good, Just Want X"  ★ Medium
"Oh okay — so the [JOB]'s going good for you? I guess, what does good actually look like for you? ...And how much are you making right now with that, if you don't mind me asking?"
"Gotcha. I mean honestly, sounds like everything's going pretty good for you — so are you not able to make more money with what you're currently doing?"
PUSH-AWAY: by acting like things sound fine, you make THEM argue why they're stuck. Push them away and they sell themselves on the problem.
Clarify Vague Words
  • "capped" → "How do you mean?"
  • "freedom" → "What do you mean by that?"
  • "stuck" → "Give me an example?"
  • "limited" → "Limited in what way?"
"I don't want to assume anything, but — what's wrong with making $[X]k a month? Is that not enough, or what's going on?"
ALT: "$7k is not bad. What makes you feel like it's not enough?"
"And I guess, you're living check to check — is that causing any problems? What does that actually look like for you?"
★ DON'T say "what negative impact is that having on you" — too salesy for low-ticket. Keep it conversational.
Path 3 — "Nothing Wrong, Just Looking"  ★ Hardest
"Yeah, no, 100% — but I guess, what's kind of been going on at the job that's having you look into other things?"
"Not making enough"
→ Goes to Path 2
"Job isn't it anymore"
→ Goes to Path 1
Still Vague
"For sure — is there any particular reason why you're not able to [make more money / hit your goals] with your current job right now?"
★ You CANNOT move forward without a tangible problem. If they won't give one, they're not buying. Keep probing or recognize the tire-kicker and fast-track to the Base 44 pitch.
Stage 4
Summarize + Transition
The "I Don't Have" Rule
Never finish with "I WANT." Always finish with "I DON'T HAVE."

❌ "So you want more freedom..."
✅ "So you're not able to travel or help your family because you're capped at $7K..."

"I want" is aspiration. "I don't have" is pain. Pain drives action.
Summarize
"Ok, so just to make sure I understand — you want to be able to [THEIR SPECIFIC DESIRE], but right now you're in [THEIR SITUATION] because of [WHAT THEY DON'T HAVE]. Is that correct?"
Income Goal Question — pick the right frame:
No Job / Replace Job
"Ok, and for you personally, how much money would you need to be making per month in order for this to be your main source of income?"
Has Job — Wants to Replace It
"Ok, and for you personally, how much money would you need to be making per month in order to fully replace your [JOB] job?"
Has Job — Life Is Good (Exception Only)
"Ok, and for you personally, how much money would you need to be making per month in order for this to be a solid side hustle for yourself?"
⚠️ EXCEPTION ONLY — use the side-hustle framing if their job/life is already going GOOD and they just wanna ADD income on top. If they need this to CHANGE their situation, push REPLACE.
Challenge Their Number
"$5k! What makes you say $5k specifically? How would that change things for you?"
Repeat back their exact number. Challenge it. Get them to paint the picture.
Stage 5
Financial Qualifying
⚠️ Longer You Stay = Worse
Every extra second raises their guard. Qualify properly but FAST. Only true DQ = <$50; everyone else, sell the appointment.
HT Qualifications
  • $2k liquid
  • — OR — minimum $4k credit limit
  • — OR — 500+ credit score, 21+, $500 budget
FQ Question
"Ok, and if we could help you [STATED GOALS]... I'm sure you're aware, just like any other business model, when it comes to e-commerce there's some startup capital involved. Just so we know what sort of strategies we can use in your case — how much capital do you have set aside at the moment to get the business up and running?"
Responses:
EZ Qualified
$ is enough and they're confident → Proceed to Stage 6
Iffy Qualified
$ is enough but "might", "maybe", "potentially" — prospect sounds unsure
Budget Too Low
More than $50, under $2k liquid
If: Iffy Qualified
"Ok, that is something we could work with, but again, if we could help you [STATED GOALS], is that [THEIR BUDGET] something you have now and would be willing to allocate if everything makes sense?"
Yes → Proceed to Stage 6
No → Not HT Qualified
→ Base44 only
If: Budget Too Low
"That's awesome! There's definitely a way you could get the business going. Just to see what sort of strategies we could use in your case, what's your age and do you have a credit card?"
"Not a big deal" tonality
21+ and has credit card → Continue
Under 21 or no credit → Not HT Qualified
→ Base44 only
"ok what's the available limit on that credit card, just to see what strategies we can use?"
Hard cash + credit limit = PIF → Proceed
21y/o+ but total = less than PIF → Check credit score
"ok, and what's your credit score?"
*US Based only — if international, budget DQ*
500+ with at least $500 hard cash → Proceed
Below 500 and/or <$500 hard cash
"awesome and have you used your credit for anything in the 4 figure range like student loans or car loan?"
Yes → Proceed to Stage 6
No → Not HT Qualified → Base44 only
<$50 / Broke — True DQ
"No worries at all bro. Go through the free course — it's yours regardless. When you're in a position to invest, hit us up."
Stage 6
Sell the Need
Knowledge Check
"Ok and it really doesn't make much difference, but just to see where you're at — what do you know about our business model so far in terms of how it works and how you actually make money with it?"
If they know nothing → give 30-sec explanation below. If they get it → skip to help question.
30-Second Explanation (If Needed)
"So basically the business model is AI ecom — you're building a real e-commerce brand — not a generic pump-and-dump store. You pick one product in an evergreen niche like health, supplements, pets, or beauty, build a real brand around it, scale it, and eventually sell it for a big payday. You're building a sellable asset. Does that make sense?"
Only if they didn't know. Keep it to 30 seconds max.
Help Question
"Awesome. So I guess based off that, what part of the business do you feel like you need the most help in? Would it be picking the right product, building the brand, or scaling with ads?"
"And why do you feel you would need the most help with that specifically?"
Their answer = the exact need you summarize back in the pitch.
Summarize the Need
"Ok, so it sounds like for you it's really just having a clear A-to-Z road map on how to [THEIR SPECIFIC NEED]. Is that fair to say?"
Get a YES before moving to the pitch. This is the need you sell to.
Stage 7
Pitch
Pitch Sequence
  1. Recommend for course → bridge to Base44
  2. Get buy-in on the course
  3. Get buy-in on Base44
  4. Get buy-in on commitment
  5. Price drop → ask for the sale
Bridge
"So next step from here would be to get you set up with the free course that you signed up for. Have you ever applied for Bronson's program in the past?"
Yes or no doesn't matter — either way, transition to the price reveal below.
Reveal
"OK, well, I'm not sure you're aware of this, but the course that we're giving to you for free used to be anywhere between $2,000 and $5,000 USD. Now we're actually able to give this away for free because we partnered with a major AI company called Base 44. Have you heard of Base 44 before or no?"
Base44 Explanation
"So here's how it actually works.

Most people who try ecom go about it all wrong — they test random product after random product, run a few stores at once, throw money at ads hopin somethin sticks, and burn more than they make back. 'Cause generic dropshipping's built to fail: no brand, no repeat customers, nothin you actually own. You're rentin a store, not buildin a business. Make sense why most people quit before they ever make a dollar?

What we do's different. Instead of guessin on a product, we use AI to research what's actually got demand — real buyers, real search — so you start on somethin proven instead of throwin darts. That alone saves you months of testin and a ton of wasted ad spend.

Then we point you to vetted suppliers, so you're not diggin through a hundred random ones hopin the quality's there. You skip the sourcing headache that stops most beginners before they even start.

And from there the AI helps at every step — findin the product, the ad copy, buildin out the brand and the store — so you move faster and cheaper than the old-school way. You don't gotta be techy, you don't need a big team, you're not figurin it out alone.

And the whole point? You're not spinnin up a throwaway store — you're buildin a real brand. Real customers that come back, somethin that's actually yours. Not a get-rich-quick thing — a real business, done the smart way.

From what we just went over — what part of that feels the most valuable to you?"
CONFIRM W/ KAUA: exact Base 44 capabilities.
★ 45-Second Rule
Never go 45 seconds in a pitch without checking in on the prospect or getting a tie-down. This should be a collaborative pitch, not you talking for 3 minutes while they say nothing.
Buy-In on Base44
"Do you actually feel like Base 44 and all the tools would help you build a real brand you could actually scale? Like, do you feel this can genuinely get you there?"
Wait for YES.
Commitment
"Awesome, well listen man — I would definitely recommend you inside the course. I'm gonna walk you through how we're gonna get started. We always want to make sure you guys actually get results, because none of this is gonna work unless you actually do what's inside. So I guess, today, are you actually committed to take action on this so that you can [THEIR DREAM OUTCOME]?"
"The only reason why I ask this is because a lot of people say they're committed, but then they'll just get inside and they don't do anything."
Why Committed
"We've worked with a lot of people, [Name]. We only want to give this to people that are actually going to use it. So I guess — why do you feel like you're committed?

Because this is a free course and the software is very cheap, so there's really not much commitment. Some people just put it on the sideline. Why is it important for you to come in and actually do this today?"
Free + cheap = low commitment frame. Makes them sell themselves on why THEY are different from the people who don't take action.
Price Reveal
"The only reason I can give you this whole course for free — the same one Bronson charged $3,000 for — is because of his direct partnership with Base 44 and Wix. You get the full A-Z course free as long as you're an active subscriber on their platforms. So you're not paying for a course, you're just covering the tools you'd need to build the brand anyway. [CONFIRM EXACT BASE 44 + WIX SUB COST] Which works better for you — paying yearly or monthly?"
Partnership frame — they're not buying software, they're maintaining access to the free program through a subscription.
Close
"Based off of those two, which one makes the most sense for you at this point? Would it be the $192 or the $50 a month?"
Shut up. Wait for the answer. Do NOT fill the silence.
Common Objections
  • "I want to try the course first" → "The course tells you what to do. Base 44 does it. Lock in the $50 now so it's there when you're ready."
  • "I need to think about it" → "What's the hesitation — the software itself or the $50?" Then handle.
  • "I can't afford it right now" → "Can you do $50 today?" If no → let them go gracefully.
  • "Is there a catch?" → "No catch. Base 44 sponsors the course because they want people using their software. You keep access as long as your subscription is active."

Sell the Need for HT
Is This Lead HT Qualified?
(from Stage 5 FQ tagging)
If: NOT HT Qualified → Wrap Up
"Alright [Name], you're all set bro. You've got the course, you've got Base 44. Go through the modules, start applying what you learn, and if you run into anything — you have my number. Just shoot me a text. Sound good?"

If: HT Qualified → Sell the Need
Pre-Frame
"Alright [Name], how are you feeling? Awesome!

Well listen bro, the only reason why we're able to actually maintain the success rate we do have in the program is because we're very intentional about the type of support and guidance we can give you guys."
Pre-frame only. Don't ask about past struggles — flow straight into the support question.
Why Simplified
Removed past-struggle question. The pre-frame alone is enough to set up the support question. The propose box already ties back to their discovery pain — we don't need a separate anchor.
Support Question
"Are you the type of person that needs coaching calls, direct support and guidance from Bronson and the team, pretty much just more one-on-one support in general?

Or what can we do to increase the probability of you succeeding in this business and ultimately getting to [DREAM OUTCOME]?"
Wait for answer → "Ok, and why do you feel like having [WHAT THEY SAID] would increase the probability of you getting to [DREAM OUTCOME]?"
Doubt the Free Course
"I mean [Name], you are in the free course right now. You could technically watch the videos and do this all by yourself.

What do you feel like you would get from having direct guidance and support from Bronson and the team that you couldn't get just doing it all alone?"
They sell themselves on needing more. Whatever they say here, you use in the propose.
★ Doubt the Free Course
Don't tell them the course isn't enough — make THEM say it. They verbalize the gap between self-paced and supported. That's the need you sell to.
Propose
"Ok listen [Name], I know you're already in the free course. And I know you said you would need more help — coaching calls, one-on-one support — to actually get to [THEIR DREAM OUTCOME], right? And that's still your biggest goal, correct?

So here's the thing bro — we just gave you the free course, but the free course is just videos. There's not one-on-one support, coaching calls — I mean it's great, but it doesn't come with what you're saying you need.

Now I only work here in the getting-started side, so when it comes to actually getting more advanced one-on-one support, I would need to connect you with one of the head coaches that works directly with Bronson. I can call Bronson and see if we could get you that additional support — I'm not sure if he could, but that coach can sit down with you and figure out if we could actually get you [THEIR SUPPORT NEED] so you can [DREAM OUTCOME].

What time today would you be sitting at your desk, ready to meet with that coach to see if we could get you the one-on-one support?"
Shut up. Wait for the time. If they give you a time → Stage 10.
Three Frames in One
  • Department Frame: "I only work here in the getting-started side" — this is a DIFFERENT call with a DIFFERENT person. Prevents them from thinking the closer call is just onboarding part 2.
  • Scarcity + Favor: "I can call Bronson and see if we could get you that" — you're doing them a favor, not selling. "I'm not sure if he could" = scarcity.
  • Clear Expectation: "That coach can sit down with you and figure out..." — they know exactly what the call is about. No bait-and-switch.
