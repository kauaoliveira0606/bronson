Pitch
Pitch Sequence
  1. Recommend for course → bridge to Base44
  2. Get buy-in on the course
  3. Get buy-in on Base44

Bridge
"So next step from here would be to get you set up with the free course that you signed up for. Have you ever applied for Bronson's course in the past?"
Yes or no doesn't matter — either way, transition to the price reveal below.
↓
Reveal
"OK, well, I'm not sure you're aware of this, but the course that we're giving to you for free used to be $3,000. Now we're actually able to give this away for free because we partnered with a major AI company called Base 44. Have you heard of Base 44 before or no?"
★ 45-Second Rule
Never go 45 seconds in a pitch without checking in on the prospect or getting a tie-down. This should be a collaborative pitch — not a 3-minute monologue while they say nothing.

"Base 44 is software that we use inside the program. The program is going to teach you two things:

1. How to find a winning product
2. How to build a successful brand around it

Let's start off with number one. When it comes to finding a winning product, most people test 20 with these pump-and-dump stores before finding something that actually works. Does that make sense so far?

The second thing I use Base 44 for is going to be to grow your business. Once you find the winning product, you're going to need to learn how to position it, how to market it, and run ads. Base 44 can literally do all of that for you. It can build you a store in 10 minutes with all of the writing and the designs, and it can run and create ads for you. We pretty much leverage AI to do like 80% of the work for us, and things that used to take months or weeks can now be cut to days or hours. And also, it'll save you thousands of dollars in the future because you're not paying for designers, product researchers, UGC creators, or anything like that.

Does that all make sense?

Awesome, I guess. From what we just went over, what do you feel like will be the most beneficial to you in terms of getting the business up and running?"

Because this is a free course and the software is very cheap, so there's really not much commitment. Some people just put it on the sideline. Why is it important for you to come in and actually do this today?"

Price Reveal
"Like I mentioned earlier, the only way we're able to give this course for free is because we partnered with Base 44. They allow us to give everything for free as long as you're an active subscriber to their platform.

There are two subscriptions:

- Annual: $192. You have access to the course and Base 44 until this time next year. Which is also going to push you to stay more committed to making this work so you can [GOALS]

- Monthly: $50 a month, which comes out to $600 a year. You'll save $400 with the annual.

You have access to the course and Base 44 as long as you're a subscriber."
↓
Close
"Based off of those two, which one makes the most sense for you at this point? Would it be the $192 or the $50 a month?"
Shut up. Wait for the answer. Do NOT fill the silence.
Common Objections
  • "I want to try the course first" → "The course tells you what to do. Base 44 does it. Lock in the $50 now so it's there when you're ready."
  • "I need to think about it" → "What's the hesitation — the software itself or the $50?" Then handle.
  • "I can't afford it" → "Can you do $50 today?" If no → Try to downsell them on other software like Wix, which is $20 a month, or Constant Contact, which is $35 a month.
  • "Is there a catch?" → "No catch. Base 44 sponsors the course because they want people using their software. You keep access as long as your subscription is active."
