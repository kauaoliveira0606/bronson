FAQ

Questions People Ask Before They Register

How long is the masterclass?

Plan for about an hour, plus live Q&A at the end. Bring your questions, because we stay on until they're answered.

Will there be a replay?

No. This is live only, and here's why: I answer questions in real time, and a recording of a Q&A isn't a Q&A. If you want in, show up Wednesday, October 28 at 7 PM EST.

Do I need a store or a product before the masterclass?

No. We start from zero. You'll see how to pick the product, build the store, and get your first sales, all inside the training.

Do you need any inventory?

No. You don't buy or store any stock upfront. Orders are fulfilled and shipped for you as customers buy, so you're never paying for product before it sells.

What do I need to have ready?

A laptop or desktop and a couple hours a day. If you can use email and browse the internet, you've got the tech skills for this.

Is this dropshipping?

No, and I want to be clear about that. I'm not teaching the Alibaba $5 product, chase-the-trend, burn-and-repeat stuff you've seen everywhere. You're building a real subscription-based e-commerce brand around a product people already buy every month. Evergreen, not trendy. Customers reorder from you, which is what makes the income compound.

I've tried e-commerce before and it didn't work. How is this different?

Most ecom models make you start from zero every month because every sale is a one-time sale. This model sells things people use up and reorder, so the same customer pays you again next month. And you're not testing 50 products hoping one sticks. One product, one brand, built to compound.

Why should I trust you?

Fair question. I built my own subscription brand from scratch while working construction and going to college, and it does over $600,000 a month in revenue now. I'm 4.7-star rated on Whop with 208 students helped, and I'm an official Shopify partner. I don't teach anything I haven't done myself.

Is this a get rich quick thing?

No, and if that's what you're looking for, this isn't for you. This is a real business that takes consistent work, usually 1 to 2 hours a day. What it gives you is a clear path instead of guessing.

How much time do I need?

About an hour or two a day. I built mine after class and after 10 to 12 hour construction shifts. That's enough when you're following a plan instead of guessing.

Do I need any tech experience?

No. AI builds the store, generates the images, and writes the copy. You make the decisions on product and brand, and the heavy lifting on the build is handled for you.

How fast until I see my first sale?
