Stage 1
Intro (Connecting Questions)
⭐ Frame — Advanced, Not Free Coaching
"Hey, you see and hear me okay? How's it goin, [Name]?"
"I'm one of the coaches here on Bronson's team."
"You got started in the Base 44 free course — how's that been goin? What've you found most helpful?"
"Just to preface — first part of this call's basic, just to understand your situation."
"What you're doin now, what you've tried, what you're lookin for — so I know the advanced side can actually help you."
"If it aligns and I can see you bein successful, I'll walk you through next steps. Sound good?"
↓
Frame = Advanced, NOT Free Coaching
Coach-on-the-team identity → acknowledge the Base 44 journey → the qualify/takeaway ("see if it aligns and I can see you being successful"). They must know this isn't the free course / a coaching call, so you never hear "y'all just pitched me $5K" at the end.
↓
"I thought this was free / coaching"
PARK, don't force. Let them run the free course, hit the wall, frame the return: "When's the best time to reconnect + check your progress?"
Stage 2
Discovery (Situation Questions)
Situation
"What was it about Bronson initially that resonated with you — enough to even hop on a call today?"
"What are you doin for a living right now?"
"And how long you been doin that?"
↓
First Questions — Then Shut Up
Get the situation + what they're chasing. This loads everything. Don't stack — one question at a time, probe each.
Stage 3
Problem Awareness — Form the Pain, Then Expand It
⭐ Problem Awareness (Form the Pain)
"How's that goin for you?"
[if they hesitate] "You don't sound too sure — anything you don't actually like about it?"
"What do you like / not like about it?"
Time-Value: "Real talk — you're puttin in 5-6 hours a day, makin about [$X] an hour?"
"Is that really what you feel you're worth?" [pause] → no → "What makes you say that?"
↓
Form the Pain First — Sit in the Pocket
Discovery ALWAYS forms pain, then expands it — that's the ammo you leverage in solution awareness + the pitch. Don't jump. The second they search for words, dig.
⭐ Expand the Pain (Gap Questions)
Gap Q's — Pull the Thread (Pick What Fits)
  • How's this affecting your life?
  • Why's this important to you?
  • How's it affecting your confidence / self-esteem?
  • Your personal life / relationships?
  • Your job / business?
  • What have you tried in the past to fix it?
  • How long has it been a struggle?
  • What's the worst thing it's caused?
  • What's it costing you (financially + emotionally)?
  • What's your biggest fear?
  • Do you have a plan to fix it?
"So after [time] at your job, not satisfied 'cause it's causin [what they said]…"
"What've you actually done to put yourself in a position to be financially free?"
"Besides more hours or a side hustle — you're still capped there. What've you started online for uncapped income?"
↓
Cost of Inaction Can Surface HERE
When they realize what it's costing them, that's a consequence moment — let it land (but don't force consequence questions everywhere; only where it's real).
Stage 4
Solution Awareness — Phase 1 (Focus on Tonality)
⭐ Solution Awareness P1
"Why not, though? What's actually prevented you from takin the first step to become [north star]?"
"Be completely real with me — I'm on your side."
If Vague ("I haven't really thought about it")
"In all due respect, that's like an overweight person saying they don't know where the gym is. So if you really thought about it — what's prevented you?" → Find the real reason (lazy / comfortable / complacent / fear).
"And what shifted now that made you want to overcome [reason] this time around?"
Grab the Real Objection (Same 4 Steps, Any Objection)
  1. "Can you see how letting [objection] hold you back has kept you stuck exactly where you don't want to be — [their spot]?"
  2. "Looking back over [time], what have you lost because of [objection]?"
  3. "So what if you keep allowing that to stop you from investing in your skillset?" → Get the consequence.
  4. "So my question isn't do you want to make [goal] — everyone does. It's how committed are you to becoming that guy and actually investing in yourself to get there… because you don't have to."
Money
"Can I share a perspective? Money's a byproduct of skills. Why do you feel you don't have the money to invest? [answer] … If you had the skill to scale a product, what would you have more of? [more money]. Two types: 'I can't so I won't' vs 'how do I get resourceful.' What prevented you from getting resourceful and doing what it took?" → grab the real objection.
Time
"Time's subjective — it comes down to what we prioritize. Why do you feel you don't have the time? … If you already had the results, would time even be an issue? Two types (I can't so I won't / how can I be resourceful and find the time). Which do you need to be? What's prevented you from prioritizing the time to change your situation?" → grab.
Laziness / Complacency
"Can I share a perspective? Laziness isn't a lack of motivation — it's not believing the effort will pay off. If you KNEW action guaranteed success, would you still hesitate? Two types: wait till they feel ready vs act despite uncertainty. What's prevented you from pushing past that pattern of hesitation?" → grab.
Cheap / Bad Past Program
"How much did you pay for it? [~$350–500] — that wasn't a mentorship. Did you actually want a mentorship? What prevented you from investing in a real one? Two types… what prevented you from getting resourceful to invest in the help you needed?" → grab.
Invested in a Previous Mentorship (Didn't Work)
"Did others in that program succeed? What was the difference — the program, or something on your end (how you showed up, the effort, execution)? Open to the idea it was the commitment on your end? What needs to change this time? Two types: full ownership vs blame external. Which do you need to be? What prevented you from being the guy who takes ownership?" → grab.
Bad Experience / Scam (Not Their Fault)
"How'd that go? Define that? What did you take away from it? Are you using it as a lesson to make better decisions moving forward — or letting it stop you from where you want to be?"
Pushback ("What I'm Doing Works")
"If it's working, keep doing it. But why do you feel doing the same thing isn't worth continuing? … What's the definition of that? [insanity]. Can you see how thinking that way for [time] has kept you from changing? How much time / opportunity have you lost? What if you never break that pattern? So how committed are you to breaking it — and why?"
Solution Awareness P2 (Double Down)
"Dude, 100% — I get why [reason] stopped you before. Cool if I give you a different way to look at it?"
"Honestly, the fear of [their excuse] is the exact thing that keeps people stuck years from now. Make sense?"
"You know what winners and losers have in common? Every human's got excuses."
"Unsuccessful people use em to stay stuck. Successful people feel the same fear and push through anyway."
"So how much longer you wanna let [their excuse] hold you back from [what they wanted]? Why? What changed?"
↓
Permission → Reframe → Identity Shift
Acknowledge + justify the concern, ask permission to reframe, then shift the identity (winners vs losers). Tonality carries it. Only double down if P1 didn't fully land.
Stage 5
Criteria → Consequences → Transition
Ideal Criteria for Help
"So for you — what would you say your ideal criteria for help is?"
"Anything else you'd be lookin for? Why's that important to you?"
Income Criteria
"Goin back to your work — would you agree you're capped, since there's only so many hours?"
"Without that limit, what's your actual ideal monthly income?"
"Is that your humble goal, or would you want more?"
"How close are you to that at your current job?"
"How long would it take you to get there? Why that long?"
"Say we help you hit [$X] in the next few months — how would that change things?" (summarize + confirm, DIG)
⭐ Consequences (Cost of Inaction)
"Say you didn't take this next step — let [the reason] stop you…"
"And you stayed where you are now for the next 1, 2, 5 years. What do you feel happens then?"
"You willin to settle for that?"
"So is it important to make this change now? Why now?"
↓
Consequence Q's Live HERE (and at Cost-of-Inaction Moments)
Don't sprinkle consequence questions everywhere — they hit hardest right here + when they realize the cost in problem/solution awareness. That's what keeps it feeling scriptless.
Transition
"The reason we have these meetings is to protect the success rate of the group."
"Only way's findin out if you're committed, coachable, and got a strong why."
"'Cause the students who carry those traits are the ones who actually see results."
