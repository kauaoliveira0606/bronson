Now let me answer the top 5 most common questions I get asked before a masterclass like this...

1) Is this dropshipping?

  • No, and I want to be clear about that.
  • I'm not teaching the $5 product, chase-the-trend, burn-and-repeat stuff you've seen everywhere.
  • You're building a real subscription-based e-commerce brand around a product people already buy every month.
  • Evergreen, not trendy. Customers reorder from you, which is what makes the income compound.

2) I tried e-commerce before and it didn't work

  • Most e-commerce models make you start from zero every month, because every sale is a one-time sale.
  • This model sells things people use up and reorder, so the same customer pays you again next month.
  • And you're not testing 50 products hoping one sticks. One product, one brand, built to compound.
  • I'll show you exactly how on the masterclass.

3) Can I do this alongside my job?

  • Yes. It takes about an hour or two a day.
  • I built mine after class and after 10 to 12 hour construction shifts.
  • That's enough when you're following a plan instead of guessing.
  • You shouldn't quit your job to start this. You can once it's out-earning your paycheck.

4) Do I need inventory or a lot of money to start?

  • No inventory. You don't buy or store any stock upfront.
  • Orders are fulfilled and shipped for you as customers buy, so you're never paying for product before it sells.
  • On the masterclass I'll show you how to get your first sales on a small budget without burning money on ads.
