How To Read Your Result

Lift

How much better or worse B did than A.

Absolute lift is the gap in percentage points. Relative lift is that gap as a percent of A.

P-value

If A and B actually performed the same, this is the chance of seeing a gap this big anyway.

Lower is better. You want it under 0.05.

Confidence

This is 1 minus the p-value. At 95% or higher, the test calls a winner.

Under that, the gap could still be noise.
